# Growth Audit — Trendy

**Live:** https://szhao-cc.github.io/trendy-flow-rebuild/ · on a phone: https://szhao-cc.github.io/trendy-flow-rebuild/?open=proto

Five ranked ideas for *Trendy: Profile Report & Boost* (SocialVibe OU), each built into a clickable prototype of the onboarding and paywall in Trendy's own design. Only the sequence and a few lines of copy change; the art direction is theirs.

| # | Idea | Screen | Potential outcome |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 1 | Give people who close the paywall one more option — Trendy PRO Monthly at $19.99, no timer, no discount | Offer on close | ARPU +10–15% |
| 2 | Put their 3-month goal in the paywall headline | Paywall | CR +15–20% |
| 3 | Ask for a rating and notifications after the paywall | Notifications | CR +10–15% |
| 4 | Swap the paywall's numbers for your real ones (4.7 from 821 ratings, 100K+ creators; no strikethrough) | Paywall | CR +10–15% |
| 5 | Let the loaders say whose profile they are reading | Profile scan, Strategy loader | CR +10–15% |

**Grounding.** Prices, ratings, quotes and feature lines come from Trendy's own App Store listing (US storefront, November 2026) and in-app screens. The profile shown in the prototype is a placeholder, not a real account. Impact ranges are Adapty's expected effect from teardowns and A/B tests across subscription apps — not measured lift for Trendy.

**Prototype features.** EN / RU / KK screens, light and an approximated dark mode, a 30-second self-playing walkthrough, jump chips for every stage, QR code, full-screen on phones.
